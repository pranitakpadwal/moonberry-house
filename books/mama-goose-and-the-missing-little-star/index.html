Mama Goose and the Missing Little Star

A tiny little star falls into a peaceful pond and finds itself far from home.

Mama Goose meets the little star and, with the help of her woodland friends, sets out to help it return to the night sky.

This beautifully illustrated picture book is a gentle bedtime story about kindness, friendship, and helping others. With simple storytelling and charming illustrations, it is perfect for quiet reading moments with little ones.

Perfect for children ages 1-5

  • Full-color illustrations on every page
  • A gentle bedtime story
  • Teaches kindness, friendship, and helping others
  • Simple, easy-to-follow storytelling for toddlers and preschoolers
  • Perfect for bedtime, story time, or family reading

Mama Goose and the Missing Little Star is a heartwarming picture book that reminds young readers that kindness and friendship can help light the way home.
