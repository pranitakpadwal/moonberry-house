Paint Your Peace: A Guided Painting Journal for Adults and Teenagers to Relax, Reflect and Reconnect with Yourself

Some feelings are difficult to put into words. Sometimes, all you need is a little time, a few colours and a space to express yourself.

Paint Your Peace is a guided painting journal created for adults who want to slow down, explore their creativity and reconnect with themselves through painting, mindful activities and gentle reflection.

Whether you enjoy painting, creative journaling or simply want to spend more time doing something meaningful for yourself, this book offers a welcoming space to express your thoughts and feelings through colour.

Explore five meaningful themes

  1. Letting Go: Explore the idea of release through calming nature-inspired illustrations and creative activities.
  2. Stress Relief: Enjoy flowing patterns and water-inspired artwork while taking a break from your everyday routine.
  3. Inner Peace: Discover moments of stillness through peaceful landscapes and soothing painting activities.
  4. Self-Love: Celebrate your individuality, recognise your qualities and express yourself through colour.
  5. Hope: Look towards new beginnings, brighter possibilities and the things you want to welcome into your life.

What's inside?

  • Guided painting activities with thoughtfully designed illustrations.
  • Creative exercises that encourage personal expression.
  • Open painting spaces where you can let your imagination lead.
  • Gentle reflection questions to explore your thoughts and experiences.
  • Before-and-after mood check-ins to reflect on your personal journey.
  • A final canvas to express who you are today.
  • Single-sided activity pages to help reduce colour transfer to the next design.

Who is this book for?

Paint Your Peace is a lovely choice for adults who:

  • Enjoy painting, colouring and creative journaling.
  • Want to make more time for personal creativity.
  • Appreciate mindful activities and reflective exercises.
  • Are looking for a relaxing alternative to screen time.
  • Enjoy thoughtful self-care activities.
  • Are searching for a creative gift for a friend, family member or themselves.

No professional art skills needed

There is no perfect way to paint, no specific colour to choose and no expectation to create something extraordinary. Every page is an opportunity to slow down and express yourself in your own way.

Take a breath. Pick your colours. Make a little space for yourself.

Your peace, your colours, your canvas.
