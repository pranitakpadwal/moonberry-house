Free printable colouring pages are a lifesaver on rainy afternoons. Finding good ones, and printing them so they colour well, takes a few simple steps.

## How do you find free colouring pages for kids?

Search for the theme your child loves, such as animals, vehicles or mandalas, and pick sites that clearly say who made the pages and how you can use them. Libraries, schools and children's museums often share free printables too. Look for clean black outlines, clear shapes and an age-appropriate level of detail.

## Check the licence and the source

- **Read the terms.** Many sites allow personal home use only, which means you cannot share or sell the pages.
- **Be careful with character pages.** Pages featuring famous cartoon characters are usually protected, so use them for personal colouring only and do not reshare them.
- **Watch for pop-ups.** Choose sites that are not crowded with ads or odd download buttons. A page should open or download as a plain image or PDF.

## How do you print colouring pages well?

1. **Choose a PDF or high-quality image.** They stay sharper than screenshots.
2. **Print at actual size.** In your print settings, pick "actual size" or "100 percent" so the page does not shrink.
3. **Pick the right paper.** Plain paper is fine for crayons. For markers or paint, use heavier paper so colour does not bleed through.
4. **Use black and white mode.** It saves coloured ink.
5. **Check the margins.** Make sure the picture is not cut off.

## Match the page to the child

Younger children enjoy big, simple shapes with thick lines. Older children often like more detail, patterns and mandalas. If a page looks frustrating, swap it for an easier one rather than pushing on.

:::tips A tidy printing routine
- Keep a folder of favourite pages
- Print a few at a time
- Store them in a clipboard or box near the crayons
- Let your child choose their own page
:::

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I find free colouring pages for kids?
Reputable sites, libraries, schools and children's museums often share free printables. Choose sources that state who made the page and how you may use it.

### Is it legal to print free colouring pages?
Printing pages for personal home colouring is usually allowed, but check each site's terms. Pages with famous characters are protected, so do not share or sell them.

### What paper is best for printing colouring pages?
Plain printer paper works for crayons and pencils. Use heavier paper for markers or paint to stop colour bleeding through.

### How do I stop printed pages being cut off?
Print at "actual size" or "100 percent" and check the print preview before printing, since "fit to page" can shrink or crop images.
