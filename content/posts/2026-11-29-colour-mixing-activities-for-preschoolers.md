There is a particular kind of wonder on a child's face when blue and yellow turn green in front of them. Colour mixing is science and art at once, and it needs very little equipment.

## What are some fun colour mixing activities for preschoolers?

Here are five that keep mess low:

1. **Zip-bag squish.** Put a blob of two paint colours in a sealed zip bag, tape it to a window or table, and let your child squish. The colours mix without touching their hands.
2. **Water droppers.** Use water tinted with food colouring, a dropper and a paper towel or coffee filter. Colours spread and blend as they soak in.
3. **Ice cube trays.** Fill the cups with tinted water and let children pour one into another to make new colours.
4. **Crayon layering.** Colour one colour lightly, then layer a second on top and see what appears.
5. **Paper plate palette.** Give them three colours of paint and a plate, and let them mix freely.

## The basic colours to start with

Red, yellow and blue are the usual starting colours. Mixed in paint:

- Red and yellow make orange
- Yellow and blue make green
- Blue and red make purple

The exact shades depend on the paints, so do not worry if the green is not perfect. The experiment is the point.

## Age-by-age steps

- **Age 2 to 3:** simply explore. They enjoy the squishing and watching change happen.
- **Age 4 to 5:** ask them to predict first: "What do you think will happen if we mix blue and yellow?"
- **Age 6 and up:** add white to make lighter tints and a touch of black for darker shades, and introduce a simple colour wheel.

:::tips Keeping mess down
- Use washable paint and an old shirt
- Work on a tray or plastic tablecloth
- Offer small amounts, not full bottles
- Keep a damp cloth within reach
:::

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you teach kids to mix colours?
Let them experiment with two colours at a time, starting with red, yellow and blue. Ask them to guess the result first, then mix and check. Keep sessions short and playful.

### What colours make orange, green and purple?
Red and yellow make orange, yellow and blue make green, and blue and red make purple. Exact shades vary with the paints.

### What is a colour wheel for kids?
It is a circle showing the main colours in order, with the mixed colours between them. Younger children enjoy making their own by mixing and painting each segment.

### What age can children start colour mixing?
Toddlers can enjoy simple mixing with supervision. Predicting results and naming mixed colours works better from around four or five.
