Privacy & cookies

Plain words about what we do, and do not do, with your information.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit themoonberryhouse.com (the "site"). The site is run by The Moonberry House ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to hello@themoonberryhouse.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask children for any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms for kids and no chat.
  • We use Google Analytics to count visits, but only if you click "Accept" in the cookie notice. If you say no, it never loads.
  • We never use your information for advertising, profiling or selling to anyone.
  • Your game progress, language and comfort settings stay on your own device.

1. Information we collect

Information you send us

If you use the "Notify me" box or email us, your email app opens and you send us a message yourself. We receive your email address and anything you write, and we use it only to reply or to tell you when something new is ready.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, may collect: the pages you view, how long you stay, the type of device and browser, your approximate location (city or country level), the website you came from, and a random identifier stored in a cookie. We use this only to understand which pages are helpful.

Stored on your device

To make the site work, we save a few small settings in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we cannot see them:

2. Cookies and how to change your mind

Strictly necessary items (the settings above) do not need consent. Analytics cookies are optional and are off until you accept. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser.

3. Children's privacy

The Moonberry House is made for children, families, and adults, and we take that seriously.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the site does not ask a child for a name, email, photo or location.
  • We do not show advertising, track children across other sites, or build profiles of children.
  • Children should use the site with a parent or carer. Only a parent or carer should accept analytics or send us an email.
  • If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it promptly.

4. How we use information

  • To reply to your messages.
  • To let you know when our books or activities are available, if you ask us to.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve them (analytics, only with consent).
  • To keep the site secure and working.

5. Who else is involved

  • GitHub (Pages) hosts the site. Like any web host, it may record technical data such as IP addresses in its logs.
  • Google provides Analytics and Tag Manager, but only after you accept.
  • Amazon sells our books. When you click "View on Amazon" you leave our site and Amazon's own privacy policy applies.
  • Instagram (Meta): if you follow our link, Instagram's own policy applies.

We do not sell your information. We share it with these services only as described above.

6. Where information is stored

Google and GitHub run servers in several countries, including outside India. By accepting analytics, you understand your analytics data may be processed outside your country.

7. How long we keep it

Emails are kept only as long as needed to reply or to keep you informed, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account and is then deleted automatically.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and (where they apply to you) laws such as the GDPR, you may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@themoonberryhouse.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

The site is delivered over HTTPS. We keep the information we hold to a minimum, which is the best protection of all. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
