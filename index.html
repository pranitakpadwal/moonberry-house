Why colour-coding?

One simple idea, four big skills

When a colour stands for a number, a letter or a feeling, the brain gets a friendly shortcut for remembering and sorting things out.

Numbers & counting

Matching numbers to colours builds number sense, one shape at a time.

Patterns & logic

Feelings & focus

For every kind of mind

Gentle by design, not as an afterthought

Children learn in very different ways. We build every activity to be calm, clear and kind, so more children can join in and feel proud.

Use the yellow Comfort button at the bottom of any page to switch on calm mode, bigger text or roomier spacing.

Moonberry House is a play-and-learning space. It is not a therapy or medical service. Our activities are meant to sit alongside the support your child already has.

  • A number on every colourWe never rely on colour alone, so colour-blind players can play too.
  • No timers, scores or buzzersNothing flashes, nothing beeps, nobody loses.
  • Calm modeSwitches off every animation and twinkle with one tap.
  • Big, forgiving buttonsEasy to tap for small hands and for limited fine-motor control.
  • Keyboard & screen-reader friendlyEverything on this page can be used without a mouse.
  • Print it and use real crayonsEvery picture can be printed blank for hands-on, off-screen colouring.
