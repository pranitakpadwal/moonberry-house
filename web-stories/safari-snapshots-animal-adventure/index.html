Safari Snapshots

Let’s meet the animals of Africa

Tap to turn the page

Grab your pretend binoculars. We are going on a safari to meet wild animals. Ready?

First stop: the golden savanna. It is wide, warm, and full of grass.

This is an elephant. Its long trunk is a nose, a hand, and a shower, all in one!

The giraffe is very tall. Its long neck helps it reach juicy leaves high in the trees.

Zebras wear stripes. Every zebra has its own pattern, a bit like your fingerprint.

Next stop: the peaceful riverbank. Shh. Let’s walk very gently.

A hippo has a big, wide mouth. It loves to cool off in the river on a hot day.

Flamingos are pink, and they often stand on one leg. Can you try that?

Now we step into the lush green forest. Look up high! Who is up in the trees?

A monkey! Monkeys love to climb. Some use their tails to help them balance.

Next, the sandy desert dunes. It is very hot here, so we walk slowly.

A camel is made for the desert. Its hump stores energy, so it can walk a long way.

Back on the savanna, we meet the lion. Lions live together in a family called a pride.

We met so many animals today! Which one is your favourite?
