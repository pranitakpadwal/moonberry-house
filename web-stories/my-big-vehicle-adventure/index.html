My Big Vehicle Adventure

Ages 2 to 8, to read together

Tap to turn the page

Vroom! Today we are going to meet all the vehicles that take us to different places.

A car takes your family on road trips. Beep beep! Where would you like to go?

A bus carries lots of people at once. Wave hello to everyone on board!

Now we are at a construction site. Builders use big, strong machines here.

A digger scoops up dirt with its big arm and bucket. Scoop, scoop, scoop!

On the farm, a tractor helps the farmer with the hard work.

A tractor is very strong. It pulls heavy things and helps grow our food.

At the railway station, people wait for the train. Listen… choo choo!

Let’s count the train cars together: one, two, three!

Next, the airport. Planes zoom down the runway and lift off!

Look up! A plane flies high above the clouds and takes people far away.

And on the open water, a boat floats along. Splash, splash!

Whenever we ride in a car, we buckle up. And near the road, we always hold a grown-up’s hand.

Can you match them? The plane flies, the boat floats, and the car drives on the road.

Colour, count, match and spot! Lots of vehicle fun is waiting inside.
