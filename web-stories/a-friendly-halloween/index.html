A Friendly Halloween

A cosy story to read together

Tap to turn the page

Come sit with me, little one. Halloween is a pretend-scary party. People dress up, and everything is just for fun.

Look at this pumpkin. Someone carved a funny face in it. It glows to say, “Happy Halloween!”

This is a friendly ghost. Under the white sheet there is only a big smile. Wave hello!

This black cat is not scary at all. She is just a kitty in a dark coat, and she loves a gentle pat.

Bats come out at night and flap up high. They are busy little animals, and they are not interested in you. Wave if you like!

The witch is a person in a costume, with a pointy hat and a broom. In stories, her pretend magic makes everything sparkle.

That old house looks spooky. But if we look closer, it is just a house with a few cobwebs and warm yellow lights.

Sometimes a spooky thing can feel a little scary. That is okay. Take my hand. Everything here is pretend, and you are safe with me.

Let’s breathe together. Breathe in slowly… now breathe out. Wow, you are so brave!

On Halloween you can dress up as anything. A witch? A bat? A pumpkin? Who would you like to be?

People share treats like candy and cupcakes. Sharing is kind. And always ask a grown-up before you eat anything.

When we go out at night, we stay close, hold hands, and look both ways. That is how we stay safe and have fun.

Halloween is for colouring, too! You can pick any colours you like. Purple pumpkins? Pink ghosts? You decide!

There is a whole book of friendly Halloween pictures waiting for you: witches, ghosts, cats, bats, castles, pumpkins, and more.

Happy Halloween! Grab your crayons and have fun.
