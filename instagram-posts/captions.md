### Posting tips
- Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags; 8–15 relevant ones works well. Putting them in the first comment is fine too.
- Post time suggestions: kids/parents posts in the early evening or on weekend mornings; adult hobby posts late evening.
- Add the link to themoonberryhouse.com in your bio, since links in captions aren't clickable.
